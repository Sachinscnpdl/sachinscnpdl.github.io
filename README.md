## About

This website presents my academic profile, research interests, selected publications, projects, and curriculum vitae.

My work focuses on **computational and data-driven materials design**, with emphasis on functional materials, multiscale simulations, phase-field modeling, finite element analysis, and machine learning-guided materials discovery.

---

## Research focus

- Multiphysics simulation of functional materials
- Phase-field modeling of microstructure evolution
- Data-driven design of multicomponent alloys
- Machine learning for piezoelectric and energy materials
- Battery interfaces, dendrite growth, and electromechanical systems
